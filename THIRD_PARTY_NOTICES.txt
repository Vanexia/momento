Momento third-party notices
===========================

Momento 0.2.4 is licensed under GNU GPL version 3. The complete licence is
installed as LICENSE. SOURCE_OFFER.txt identifies the matching source archives
published beside the installer on the Momento 0.2.4 GitHub release:

  Momento-0.2.4-source.zip
  Momento-0.2.4-third-party-source.zip

The third-party source bundle contains the exact hash-verified source inputs
and native build records for Momento's FFmpeg helper, Momento's minimal
FFmpeg/PyAV 17.0.1 recording runtime, and the PyQt/Qt multimedia runtime.
BUILD_INFO.txt records binary identities, hashes, build records, and
provenance.

This application is built with open-source components, including:

- PyQt6 and SIP, distributed under GPLv3 for this build.
  https://riverbankcomputing.com/software/pyqt/
- Qt 6 libraries, distributed under their applicable GPL/LGPL terms.
  The source bundle includes Qt Base, SVG, Multimedia, and Translations 6.11.1,
  plus FFmpeg 7.1.3 and zlib 1.3.1 used by Qt Multimedia. Unused Qt PDF
  components are excluded from the application.
  https://www.qt.io/licensing/open-source-obligations
- FFmpeg 8.1.2, built as Momento's minimal LGPL helper from the checked-in
  reproducible recipe. Its licence and complete build identity are installed
  under licenses/FFmpeg.
  https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.1.2.tar.xz
  https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
- PyAV 17.0.1 with Momento's reproducible minimal FFmpeg 8.0.1 recording
  runtime. The checked-in build recipes retain H.264/AAC recording and Momento's
  NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Media Foundation, and libx264 encoder paths. The matching
  source bundle includes PyAV, FFmpeg, x264, NVIDIA codec headers, AMD AMF
  headers, Intel oneVPL, GCC, and winpthreads. Unused codec and runtime
  libraries from the general-purpose PyAV wheel are not distributed.
  https://pyav.org/
  https://ffmpeg.org/
- CPython 3.12 and the PyInstaller bootloader.
- windows-capture declares OpenCV for optional image saving. Momento does not
  use that API and excludes OpenCV and its FFmpeg DLL from the public binary.
- Plus Jakarta Sans, copyright its project authors and licensed under the SIL
  Open Font License 1.1. The font licence is bundled beside the font files.
- PyAV, NumPy, psutil, PyAudioWPatch/PortAudio, windows-capture, and their
  transitive dependencies.

Distributor builds that enable YouTube additionally include requests, urllib3,
google-api-python-client, google-auth, google-auth-oauthlib,
google-auth-httplib2, httplib2, cryptography, and their transitive dependencies.

Copyright remains with each component's authors. Their licence files are
included under licenses/Python/<distribution>-<version>, preserving paths from
the exact locked Python distributions. Packaging fails if a locked
distribution supplies no licence or notice file. CPython's licence is installed
separately under licenses/CPython. This notice does not replace those texts.

The standard Momento public build does not include a Google OAuth client
identity or any developer/user account data. YouTube upload is available only
in distributor builds that deliberately provide an approved OAuth identity.
